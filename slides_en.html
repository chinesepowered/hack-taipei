The phone call every family dreads

Today

「阿嬤，我是你孫子，我出事了，今天一定要匯 30 萬。」 "Grandma, it's me. I'm in trouble. I need NT$300,000 today."
  1. She panics and heads to the ATM.
  2. The bank app warns her in words she doesn't understand.
  3. The family finds out tomorrow.

With Grandma's Wallet

  1. She puts the agent on speaker. It listens to the call with her.
  2. Scam Shield scores the story against 165 fraud patterns and pauses the payment. The agent explains why, in her language, and offers to call her grandson.
  3. Her family gets a one-tap request. The on-chain wallet will not release funds without them.
